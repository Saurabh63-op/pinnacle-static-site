Writer's Choice

Advanced Writing Masterclass | Essays, Op-Eds, Portfolios & Long-Form Narratives

Ask For Course Details

Who This Program is for ?

  • • Aspiring authors, essayists, content creators, and journalists
  • • College students and professionals crafting high-stakes essays or articles
  • • Adults wanting to hone a distinctive, commanding written voice

How the Program Works

  • • Intensive critique circles and deep-dive editorial feedback
  • • Analysis of classic essays, investigative journalism, and memoirs
  • • Portfolio-driven curriculum tailored to your personal writing goals

What You Will Work On

  • • Rhetorical devices, hooks, and argument architectures
  • • Developing a sharp, memorable author voice
  • • Ruthless self-editing and structural restructuring
  • • Pitching publications and creating portfolio-ready work

Level Structure & Learning Outcomes

Module 1 - Voice & Rhythm

Sentence architecture, personal voice, voice consistency

Outcome: Develop a distinct signature writing style

Module 2 - Non-Fiction & Essays

Crafting persuasive essays, analytical papers, and op-eds

Outcome: Write sharp, structured, compelling non-fiction

Module 3 - Narrative Architecture

Long-form pacing, narrative tension, thematic depth

Outcome: Structure multi-chapter stories or long articles

Module 4 - Publication Portfolio

Refining 3 major pieces, querying editors, submissions

Outcome: Graduate with a submission-ready literary portfolio

Transform your writing from ordinary draft to publication-worthy craft.
